# Provider artwork

The assistant's provider menu uses bundled artwork to identify the selected service. It does not fetch logos while the app runs. The provider name distinguishes ChatGPT sign-in from the OpenAI API connection even though both use the OpenAI blossom.

- **ChatGPT and OpenAI:** existing original black and white OpenAI artwork, with provenance and hashes in [ChatGPT artwork](chatgpt-branding.md).
- **Claude:** the original 24, 48, and 72-pixel macOS template images distributed with Claude desktop 4.4212.0. The files are unchanged, with AppKit's template rendering selecting the native foreground color for light and dark appearances. [Claude desktop](https://claude.com/download), [Anthropic trademark guidelines](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/trademark-guidelines).
- **DeepSeek:** the original 225-pixel whale favicon from [DeepSeek's website](https://www.deepseek.com/favicon.ico), downloaded November 3, 2026. The icon was converted from ICO to PNG without resizing or changing its colors.

The marks belong to their respective owners and identify the service connection; they do not imply endorsement of Tern. They remain subject to the owners' terms rather than Tern's software license.
