# Organizing mail

Drag one message or a selection from the mail list onto a sidebar mailbox or a favorite.

| Destination | Result |
|---|---|
| A label | Moves mail out of Inbox and the source label into the destination label. Other tags stay attached. |
| A label while holding Option | Adds the label and keeps mail in its current mailbox. |
| Trash | Moves mail to Trash. |
| Inbox | Returns mail to Inbox, including mail in Trash or Spam. |
| All Mail | Archives mail. Mail moved from Trash or Spam becomes visible again. |
| Starred, Unread, Important, or an inbox category | Applies that status or category without moving mail. |

Gmail labels can serve as folders or tags. Their icons are cosmetic; Option determines whether a drag adds a tag or moves mail.

Account-specific destinations accept mail from that account. A selection spanning accounts can use a combined mailbox such as Trash. Dropping it on one account's label is rejected as a whole.

Folder moves, label changes, and trash actions offer Undo. Return snoozed mail to Inbox before moving it to a label or archiving it. Option-drag can tag mail while retaining its snooze.

Right-click a message to reply, forward, organize, change read/star/importance status, report spam, restore it, or open and copy its links. The menu targets the clicked row or selected group. When the list shows individual messages, actions affect only those messages. Available actions depend on the mail.

Right-click sidebar mailboxes, labels, saved searches, or Favorites for their management actions. Trash and Spam expose permanent deletion through the existing review and permission flow. Calendar events, tasks, and newsletter rows also expose their existing actions through context menus.
