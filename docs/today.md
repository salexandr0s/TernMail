# Today

Open **Today**, the third option in the workspace picker, or choose it from the **Go** menu. The page brings together to-dos, a small calendar and day agenda, unread inbox mail, and follow-up conversations. Narrow windows stack the sections vertically.

Choose a task account and list in the to-do section. Calendar selection is independent of the main Calendar page. Unread mail comes from the available cache; the page identifies limited or unavailable coverage. Displaying a message here does not mark it read.

Follow-ups use the current account profile and include automatically tracked unanswered sent conversations and commitments you explicitly track. Configure automatic reminders and optional draft preparation in **Settings → Follow-ups**. Today does not claim to find every outstanding obligation in your mailbox.

Each follow-up appears once, with its reasons:

- **Needs attention** includes work you owe, deadlines due today or overdue, and due reminders.
- **Coming up** shows other deadlines in the next seven calendar days.
- **Waiting for** shows remaining requests to other people. A recorded reply leaves the commitment open until you complete or dismiss it.
- **Since your last visit** shows changed records that do not already appear above. Other rows carry their change explanations in place.

Search filters Today independently of Waiting for. Open the source email, edit the commitment, prepare a follow-up draft, complete it, or dismiss it from its row. These actions use the same record as Waiting for. A draft still needs review and sending in the composer.

## Snooze and restore

Snooze a commitment until tomorrow morning, Friday morning, or a chosen date and time. Snooze works even without a reminder. It hides the item from the regular briefing and postpones its reminder alerts without changing its deadline or marking the work complete. Reply checks continue.

Use **Snoozed** to see deferred commitments and bring one back early. Waiting for also keeps the record visible. When the snooze expires, the item returns to the briefing even if notifications are off.

## Visit history

Today remembers the work presented during a visit. On the first visit it establishes a baseline; later visits explain changes such as a new deadline, a received reply, or completion. Notification delivery alone is not a change. Each account profile has its own history.

Changes hidden by search, snooze, or a closed window are not marked as seen. If history cannot be loaded or saved, Today reports the problem while leaving current work usable. Removing an account removes its local follow-ups and visit history.

See [Waiting for and follow-ups](follow-ups.md) for reminder conditions and local execution limits.
